Personal Backup

A private desktop backup application for the owner's computer. It is intended to use restic and rclone to store and restore encrypted backup snapshots in the owner's Google Drive account.

This public site provides application information only. It does not sign in to Google, accept uploads or offer a hosted backup service.

How it works

Restic encrypts backup data on the computer before rclone transfers it to Google Drive. The intended OAuth permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which limits access to files created by or explicitly made available to the application. The connection is used to create, read, update and remove backup objects as needed for backup, integrity checking, restoration and retention.

Use and access

The application is intended solely for its owner and is not offered to the general public. Setup and restoration are performed on the owner's computer. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.

Questions about this information site can be submitted through the public issue tracker. Do not post personal information or credentials there.
